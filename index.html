Method & honest caveats

How it's measured

  • Collect every CERT-In Vulnerability Note for the year; extract its publish date and CVE IDs.
  • Download CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog — CVEs confirmed exploited in the wild, with the date each was added.
  • Match by CVE ID. Lag = CERT-In advisory date − CISA KEV date. Keep CERT-In's first mention of each CVE.

What the numbers actually say

CERT-In is fast on average — median lag near zero, sometimes warning before CISA. The story isn't "India is slow." A minority of cases run a week or more late, concentrated in specific vendors.
The matched set is small (CVEs in both CERT-In notes and CISA KEV). Per-vendor medians rest on a handful of CVEs each — directional, not statistically strong. Running 2023–2024 widens the base.
